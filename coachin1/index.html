Clients log workouts and meal deviations; you see them in their profile.

Legal & Privacy

🔒 Privacy Policy

Who we are

Micro Macro Nutri Labs (Martin Fabian, see Impressum below) operates the CoachIn1 mobile application ("the App"). The App connects coaches with their clients. This policy explains what data we collect, who can see it, how we use it, and your rights.

Data we collect

  • Account information – your email address, your name, and a password. Passwords are stored only in hashed form by our authentication provider and are never visible to us.
  • Client details entered by coaches – when a coach adds a client, the coach provides the client's name and email address. This is used to send the client an invitation email and to link the client to that coach.
  • Meal plans – meals and foods with amounts, calories, protein, carbohydrates and fat, created by coaches and assigned to clients.
  • Training plans – training sessions, exercises, sets, rep ranges, cardio duration, intensity and notes, created by coaches and assigned to clients.
  • Workout logs – workouts clients record, including date, exercises, sets, repetitions, weights, cardio activities, durations and notes.
  • Meal logs – foods clients record as skipped or eaten in addition to their plan, with optional calories and notes.
  • Authentication tokens – short-lived session tokens that keep you signed in. They are stored encrypted on your device.
  • Technical logs – our hosting provider automatically records technical data such as IP addresses and request timestamps when the App talks to our servers, for security and troubleshooting.

Workout and meal logs can reveal information about your health and fitness. We process this data only to provide the App's coaching features and only with your consent, which you give by entering it.

Data we do NOT collect

  • We do not access your location, contacts, camera, photos or microphone.
  • We do not use advertising or analytics SDKs, and we do not track your activity across other apps or websites.
  • We do not collect payment card or banking information. Any payment arrangement between a coach and a client happens outside the App.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for marketing purposes.

Who can see your data

  • If you are a client, every coach you are linked to can see your name, email address, the plans they assigned to you, and your workout and meal logs.
  • If you are a coach, your name and the plans you assign are visible to the clients you assign them to.
  • No one else using the App can see your data. Access is enforced on the server for every request.

How we use your data

  • To create and secure your account and keep you signed in.
  • To send invitation, confirmation and password emails.
  • To store, sync and display plans and logs between coaches and their clients.
  • To keep the service secure and fix technical problems.

Legal bases (GDPR)

  • Performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) – providing the App and your account.
  • Consent (Art. 6(1)(a) and Art. 9(2)(a) GDPR) – workout and meal logs that may contain health-related data. You can withdraw consent at any time by deleting the data or your account.
  • Legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR) – security and technical logs.
  • Coaches who enter a client's name and email are responsible for having a lawful basis to do so, typically their existing coaching relationship with that client.

Third-party services

The App uses the following service providers, each governed by their own privacy policies:

  • Supabase (Supabase, Inc.) – database hosting, user authentication, server functions and transactional emails (invitations, confirmations, password resets). All App data described above is stored with Supabase.
  • Google Play (Google LLC) – distribution of the App on Android.

Where a provider processes data outside the European Economic Area, the transfer is protected by the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses or an equivalent safeguard.

Data retention & deletion

  • Your data is kept for as long as you have an account.
  • When a coach removes a client, the plans that coach made for the client are deleted. If the client is not linked to any other coach, the client profile and all of the client's workout and meal logs are deleted as well.
  • To delete your account and all associated data, use the account deletion page or contact us at the address below. Deletion is completed within 30 days.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict and port your data, to object to processing, and to withdraw consent at any time. To exercise these rights, contact us at the address below. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority.

Children's privacy

The App is not directed at children under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date below will be updated accordingly. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance.
